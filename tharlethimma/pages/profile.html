My Profile

Meet Thimma, Your Host!

Hello! I'm Thimma, a passionate storyteller and a connoisseur of all things humorous. My journey into podcasting began with a simple desire to share laughter and lighthearted perspectives on life's everyday adventures. I love exploring quirky observations, personal anecdotes, and sometimes, just plain absurdities that are guaranteed to bring a smile to your face. When I'm not behind the microphone, you can find me exploring local cafes, binge-watching classic comedies, or attempting to perfect my (terrible) stand-up routine in front of my mirror. Join me on "Tharle Thimma" as we navigate the amusing side of life together!
